Why Holland Boats

Craft first. Everything else follows.

We keep every build small enough to touch by hand — from the first pencil line on the hull drawing to the sea trial before delivery.

Hand-fitted construction

Hulls are laid, faired, and finished by a crew of eleven boatbuilders — no outsourced mouldings, no shortcuts on the joinery.

Dutch design heritage

Hull lines drawn from three generations of waterway boatbuilding — stable in chop, quiet at cruise, easy to handle solo.

Every boat sea-trialed

No yacht leaves the yard until it's run in open water and signed off by the same builder who fitted the engine.

How a Build Happens

From hull drawing to harbor

Every custom build follows the same four stages, with you involved at each one.

01

Design

We work from your itinerary, not a catalogue — layout, power, and finish are set together.

02

Build

Hull, joinery, and systems are fitted in sequence by the same small crew, start to finish.

03

Sea Trial

Open-water testing and a full systems check before anything is signed off.

04

Delivery

Handover at the yard or your home harbor, with a full walkthrough of every system.
