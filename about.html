Three generations of Dutch boatbuilding

Holland Boats started as a two-man repair shed on the Loosdrecht lakes. It's grown into a full yard — but the way we build hasn't changed much.

Our Story

Built from a repair shed on the Loosdrecht lakes

In 1987, Willem Holland began repairing sailing dinghies and small launches out of a rented shed on the Loosdrecht lakes. Word got around: the repairs held up, and the small modifications he made to hull shapes made boats easier to handle in the area's short, choppy chop.

By the mid-1990s, owners were asking him to build them something new rather than fix what they had. The first Holland-built hull launched in 1994. Today the yard is run by Willem's daughter, Anneke Holland, with a crew of eleven builders — most of whom trained on-site.

We've stayed small on purpose. Every hull that leaves the yard has been touched by a crew who could tell you, from memory, which boat it was and who it's going to.

Milestones

Nearly four decades on the water

1987

The repair shed opens

Willem Holland begins repairing dinghies and small launches on the Loosdrecht lakes.

1994

First Holland-built hull launches

A 26-foot day boat, built for a repeat repair customer, becomes the first full Holland Boats build.

2003

The Zuiderzee line debuts

Our first cabin cruiser model, designed for multi-day trips on open water.

2011

Yard expansion at Kortenhoef

A move to a larger waterside yard allows two hulls to be under construction at once.

2019

Anneke Holland takes the helm

Second-generation leadership, with the founding crew's building methods carried forward.

2023

Hybrid-electric drive option introduced

A diesel-electric hybrid package becomes available across the Zuiderzee and IJssel lines.

What We Hold To

The values behind the build

Craftsmanship over speed

We build two to three hulls at a time, not twelve. Every joint gets checked by a second set of hands.

Materials that outlast trends

Marine-grade timber joinery and hand-laid glasswork, chosen to be repaired rather than replaced.

Built to be owned for decades

We keep build records for every hull, so a boat we launched in 1998 can still be serviced to spec today.
