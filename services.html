Custom Build Process

What happens after you call

Step 1

Consultation & design

We talk through how you use the water, then draw a layout, hull spec, and power option to match. Usually 2–4 weeks.

Step 2

Contract & scheduling

A fixed build slot is reserved at the yard, with a payment schedule tied to build milestones, not the calendar.

Step 3

Hull & systems build

You're welcome at the yard throughout — most owners visit two or three times during construction to see progress firsthand.

Step 4

Sea trial & handover

Open-water testing, a full systems walkthrough, and delivery at the yard or your home harbor.

Good to Know

Common questions

How long does a custom build take?

Most builds run 8–14 months from signed contract to delivery, depending on model and current yard capacity.

Do you service boats you didn't build?

Yes — restoration, maintenance, and storage are open to any comparable motor yacht or day boat, not just Holland-built hulls.

Can I visit during construction?

Absolutely. Most owners visit the yard two or three times over the build to see the hull in progress.

What's included in winter storage?

Haul-out, covered storage, and a spring launch check are included; winterizing and mechanical work are quoted separately.

Not sure which service you need?

Send us a few details about your boat or your plans and we'll point you the right way.
