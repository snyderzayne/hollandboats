Why Holland's

Built to be used, not just admired

We build a small lineup of proven hulls instead of a big catalog of unfamiliar ones — so every boat benefits from decades of the same molds, the same jigs, and the same hands.

Hulls we know by heart

Five models, each built from molds and jigs that have been refined over years — not a new design every time.

Kit or finished

Buy a hull and deck kit and finish it yourself, or have us deliver a boat that's ready to launch, tested, and sea-trialed.

A Maine shop, start to finish

Glassing, assembly, rigging, and launch all happen in Belfast — including the sea trial, which we're there for.
